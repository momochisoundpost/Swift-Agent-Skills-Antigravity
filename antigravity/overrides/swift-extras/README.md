# Swift Extras: Alternatives (Community)

Alternative community skills for Swift Concurrency, Swift Testing, SwiftData, and Apple platform accessibility auditing (SwiftUI, UIKit, and AppKit).

> [!NOTE]
> This is an unofficial community port for Google Antigravity. All bundled skills remain MIT-licensed by their respective upstream authors (see `NOTICE.md`).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Overlap Notice:** This pack intentionally overlaps in topic coverage with `swift-core` and `swift-ui-craft` (specifically **Swift Concurrency**, **Swift Testing**, **SwiftData**, and **Accessibility**). To avoid redundant context or conflicting conventions, consider enabling one preferred author per topic rather than activating overlapping skills simultaneously.
